# Connect music services

`/setup` is optional. Read `.cursor/skills/start-here/SKILL.md` in full and follow its **Commands** section for the service the creator wants to connect. Reuse their existing album and connections.

For Suno Free, use the signed-in Suno browser. If they need to sign in or sign up, follow **Suno account** in `start-here`. Recommend phone, and fill January 1, 1995 when Suno requires a birth date. A working session skips that menu. For a paid Suno plan, use the official connector and check what their account can already do. Connect Cursor when the brief and track names are ready and the creator is about to compare prompts, or when they explicitly ask for that connection. If a provider needs sign-in or approval for a purchase, let the creator complete it on the provider’s page. Resume their album after the requested connection works.

The first playable take goes into `Open Album.html`. Follow the local preview and sharing steps in `start-here`.
