## Problem

G and H came closest and still missed. They play the hook about every four bars and take too long to reach the floor. p4 asked for that with two "do not"s. Suno ignored both.

The empty space had no owner. Ask for "sparser" and the model refills the hole with the last melody it heard, which is the trumpet. One hand-written p5 counted the hook and put the kit in at bar eight. That is not five paid runs.

This suite is still a Cover of Gauc take A. Not a second Leek Freak. Not Blank Create.

## Rejected

- The hand-written p5 as a suite. One map, not five lanes.
- C5-P5 open bell. Mute comes off and it reads as a fanfare, which is how this title goes cartoon.
- C5-P4 delay tail. Pretty idea. It would smear the dry 808 the source owns.
- C2-P3 single statement in the last third. Plants nothing. Stops being a Cover of this hook.
- C3 808-alone eight-bar opens. That is Leek Freak, and p1 already lost the floor that way.
- Plunger mute. Wah on a joke title.
- Festival main-stage visuals.
- C1-P1 black car at a service entrance. Leek Freak already has a black car idling in a loading dock.
- Continuous hand-wah (C2-P4 alternative). Turns restraint back into a busy lead.
