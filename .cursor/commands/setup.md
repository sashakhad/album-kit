# Connect music services

`/setup` is optional. Read `.cursor/skills/start-here/SKILL.md` in full and follow its **Commands** section for the service the creator wants to connect. Reuse their existing album and connections.

For Suno Free, use the signed-in Suno browser. For a paid Suno plan, use the official connector and check what their account can already do. Connect Cursor when the creator chooses Full Setup for a model comparison. If a provider needs sign-in or approval for a purchase, let the creator complete it on the provider’s page. Resume their album after the requested connection works.

The first playable take goes into `Open Album.html`. Follow the local preview and sharing steps in `start-here`.
